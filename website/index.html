Straight answers

Before you put it on the tab.

Is Pizza Promo Pro a browser application?

No. It is a native Electron desktop application designed to run locally on your computer.

Does it include an AI subscription?

No. You choose and connect a supported AI provider, or use Ollama locally. Any provider usage charges are paid directly to that provider.

Can it connect to my social accounts?

Direct account access depends on each platform’s supported API and authorization process. Pizza Promo Pro keeps those connections separate, shows their capability clearly, and provides a manual handoff path when direct publishing is not available.

Does it create images and video?

Pizza Promo Pro can send supervised image and video jobs to Higgsfield using your connected account. You choose the model, review the prompt and estimated credits, optionally select compatible reference images, and approve the spend before generation begins.

Can it import my existing menu?

It can preview possible items from public Clover, Square, Slice, Toast, and other compatible menu pages. Some sites may restrict automated access or require a manual import. Nothing is saved until you review the preview.

Is this another monthly subscription?

No. Pizza Promo Pro is planned as a one-time software purchase. Optional outside services may charge their own fees.

What happens after the first year?

The version you purchased keeps working. You are never forced into a Pizza Promo Pro subscription. Future major-version upgrades are optional and planned at $99.

How many shops and computers are included?

The founding and standard licenses cover one shop or location and activation on up to two computers. A $499 multi-location license for up to three locations is planned after that workflow is verified.
